About Chomene

Built around practical progress.

Chomene Investment Limited is a Zambian company working across construction materials, full-scale construction, vehicle care and modern IT solutions.

Who we are

A broad capability with a focused purpose.

Our different service lines are connected by one idea: help people and organisations move important work forward with less friction.

That can mean sourcing materials for a build, coordinating a construction project, keeping a vehicle dependable, or improving the technology a business relies on. We approach each request by first understanding the intended outcome.

Our visionEnable businesses and communities through reliable infrastructure, mobility and digital innovation.
Our approachListen carefully, define the requirement and recommend a practical next step.
Our baseLusaka, Zambia, with enquiries welcomed from across the country.
Our structureSpecialised service areas connected under one company.
What guides us

Principles for doing useful work.

These principles shape how the team communicates, scopes requests and supports clients.

Integrity

Be honest about what a request needs and what the next step should be.

Attention

Understand the context before proposing products, work or technical choices.

Collaboration

Keep communication open between the client and the people handling the work.

Progress

Focus on tangible outcomes that support the client's broader objective.
