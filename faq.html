Which services does Chomene provide?
Chomene works across four areas: construction materials supply, full-scale construction, general hardware materials and modern IT solutions. The services page lets you filter and compare them.
How do I request a quote or booking?
Open the enquiry form, select the closest service and describe what you need. The individual service pages also link to the form with the correct service preselected.
What information should I include?
Include the intended outcome, location, preferred timing and any known quantities or specifications. Hardware enquiries should include item sizes, finishes and amounts. IT enquiries should explain the users, current setup and desired outcome.
Can I enquire if I do not have a full specification?
Yes. Describe the need in plain language and include the details you do know. The first conversation can help clarify what information is still needed.
Where is Chomene based?
Chomene is based in Lusaka, Zambia. A precise street address is not published on this website, so please contact the team before planning a visit.
What areas do you serve?
The company is based in Lusaka and welcomes enquiries from across Zambia. Whether a specific request can be supported will depend on its service type, location and scope.
How quickly will I receive a response?
Response timing can depend on the detail and service area involved. For urgent matters during business hours, call +260 976 898 797.
How is information from the enquiry form used?
The details are used to review and respond to your request. See the privacy policy for information about form processing and third-party services used by the site.
