Privacy

How website information is handled.

This page explains what information may be collected when you use the Chomene website and enquiry form.

InformationHow it is usedExternal servicesRetentionYour choicesContact

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Information you provide

When you submit the enquiry form, you may provide your name, email address, phone number, selected service, subject and message. You choose what to include in the message, so please avoid sending sensitive personal information that is not necessary for the enquiry.

How information is used

Information submitted through the site may be used to:

  • Review and respond to your enquiry;
  • Understand the service, project or booking you are asking about;
  • Communicate with you about the request; and
  • Maintain reasonable records of business correspondence.

External services used by this site

The enquiry form is sent using FormSubmit, a third-party form-processing service. The site also loads resources from Google Fonts, Cloudflare's cdnjs service, Unsplash and Google Maps. When these resources load, those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser details and the page requested, according to their own policies.

If you prefer not to use the form, you can contact Chomene directly by phone or email.

Retention and security

Enquiry information should be kept only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage the related business communication, and meet applicable record-keeping obligations. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You may ask about personal information you submitted through the site, request a correction, or ask that it be deleted where appropriate. Contact the company using the details below and provide enough context to identify the relevant communication.
